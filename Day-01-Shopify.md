# Day 1 — Shopify & E-commerce Basics

## 📅 Overview

Today I explored Shopify and learned the basics of how an e-commerce store works. I also explored Shopify's analytics and learned important e-commerce and marketing terms.

## 🛍️ Shopify

- Explored the Shopify platform.
- Created a sample e-commerce website.
- Added products and explored the different product management options.

## 📦 Simple Sample Data

- Installed the **Simple Sample Data** app.
- Used the app to generate dummy/sample data for the store.
- Explored how sample data can be useful for testing and understanding an e-commerce store.

## 📊 Analytics & Reports

- Explored the **Analytics** section in Shopify.
- Opened the **Reports** section.
- Checked several reports to understand what information they provide.
- Learned how reports help a store owner understand the performance of the store.

## 🔎 Analytics & Exploration

- Explored the **Exploration** section.
- Learned about **Metrics**.
- Understood that metrics allow us to select specific data or numbers that we want to analyze.

## 📚 E-commerce & Marketing Terms

Learned 28 important e-commerce and marketing terms, including:

- Gross Sales
- Net Sales
- And other important sales, marketing, and business metrics.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- Shopify can be used to build and manage an e-commerce store.
- Sample data can be generated for testing and learning.
- Reports help us understand store performance.
- Metrics allow us to focus on specific data.
- Understanding business and marketing terms is important when working with e-commerce analytics.

## ✅ What I Learned Today

1. Basic Shopify store setup and management.
2. How to generate dummy data using an app.
3. How Shopify Reports work.
4. What metrics are and why they are useful.
5. Important e-commerce and marketing terminology.
